Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 9, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use Air, we collect the following types of information:

  • Account information: name, email address, phone number, and profile photo
  • Listing information: property details, descriptions, images, and pricing provided by hosts
  • Booking information: dates, guest counts, and booking history
  • Payment information: processed securely by Stripe — we do not store card details
  • Usage data: pages visited, search queries, and interactions with the platform

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and improve the Air platform
  • Process bookings and payments
  • Send booking confirmations and service notifications
  • Respond to customer support requests
  • Detect and prevent fraud or abuse
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information with:

  • Hosts: when you make a booking, we share your name and contact details with the host
  • Guests: hosts' public listing information is visible to all users
  • Service providers: Firebase (database), Stripe (payments), Google Places (hotel search) — each under their own privacy policies
  • Law enforcement: when required by applicable law or legal process

4. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase, which employs industry-standard encryption at rest and in transit. Profile images and listing photos are stored in Firebase Storage. We retain your data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services.

5. Cookies

Air uses session cookies to keep you logged in and to maintain your preferences. We do not use third-party advertising cookies. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but this may affect platform functionality.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate information via your profile settings
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Withdraw consent for non-essential data processing

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@air-reservations.com.

7. Children's Privacy

Air is not intended for users under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that a child has provided us with personal information, we will delete it immediately.

8. Third-Party Links

Our platform may contain links to third-party websites, including hotel booking sites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites and encourage you to review their privacy policies.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by posting a notice on our platform or sending an email to your registered address.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at privacy@air-reservations.com.
